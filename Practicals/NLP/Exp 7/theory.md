Part-of-Speech (POS) Tagging
POS Tagging is the process of assigning a grammatical category to each word according to its meaning and context.
Common POS categories include:

- NOUN – Noun
- PROPN – Proper Noun
- VERB – Verb
- ADJ – Adjective
- ADV – Adverb
- PRON – Pronoun
- DET – Determiner
  For example:
  “John plays football.”
  John → Proper Noun
  plays → Verb
  football → Noun
  POS tagging helps an NLP system understand the grammatical structure of a sentence.
  Named Entity Recognition (NER)
  Named Entity Recognition identifies important real-world entities in text and assigns them predefined categories.
  Common entities include:
- PERSON – Person
- ORG – Organization
- GPE – Country, city or state
- DATE – Date
- TIME – Time
- MONEY – Monetary value
  For example:
  “John works at Microsoft in Mumbai.”
  John → PERSON
  Microsoft → ORG
  Mumbai → GPE
  POS Tagging vs NER
  POS Tagging identifies the grammatical role of a word, whereas NER identifies its real-world entity type.
  For example, Mumbai is identified as a Proper Noun (PROPN) by POS tagging and as a Geographical Entity (GPE) by NER.
  Applications
  POS Tagging and NER are useful in:
- Information Extraction
- Search Engines
- Chatbots
- Question Answering
- Machine Translation
- Text Summarization
- Document Analysis
